# IsoCourt writing voice

Derived from the landing page and product UI. Use this for FAQ, glossary, and any new marketing copy.

## Who you are
A sharp club player who built the tool. You talk like you're between games, not pitching a Series A.

## Rules
- Short sentences. Contractions. Second person ("you").
- Name the pain before the feature.
- Active voice.
- No em dashes. Use commas, periods, colons, or a line break.
- No "exciting", "thrilled", "game-changing", "seamless", "leverage", "utilize".
- No textbook openers ("In today's fast-paced world").
- Jargon only if you define it in the next breath, or link the glossary.
- IsoCourt = the product. Birdzo = the AI coach persona inside it. Don't treat them as two companies.

## Jargon → plain
| Avoid bare | Prefer |
|------------|--------|
| pose estimation | pose tracing (skeleton overlaid on your body) |
| inference | analysis / reading the clip |
| LLM | coaching tips written from the stroke scores |
| CNN-LSTM | our stroke model (link research later) |
| quality_numeric | score out of 10 |

> IsoCourt leverages cutting-edge computer vision to deliver seamless biomechanical insights that empower athletes to optimize performance.

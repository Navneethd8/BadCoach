  \caption{\textbf{JVC overview.}
    A hit-centered RGB clip $\mathbf{V}$ and skeleton clip $\mathbf{S}$ are encoded into
    patch tokens $\mathbf{Z}_v$ and joint tokens $\mathbf{Z}_s$.
    Joint tokens cross-attend to visual patches ($\mathbf{Q}$ from $\mathbf{Z}_s$,
    $\mathbf{K},\mathbf{V}$ from $\mathbf{Z}_v$), then a divided spatiotemporal
    transformer and contact-weighted temporal pooling produce a clip embedding
    $\mathbf{f}$, which is classified into one of nine stroke types.
    $T{=}16$ frames per hit; skeletons from MediaPipe.}
